Privacy policy

Work Invoices Backup is a personal desktop backup workflow. With the operator’s authorization, rclone uses read-only Google Drive access to list files and read document contents in the configured invoice folder. It uses this data to export Google Sheets invoices to Excel and copy invoice files to the operator’s Windows computer for offline use.

The workflow stores those copies locally and keeps its Google authorization in the local rclone configuration. It does not upload invoice data to this website, sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with other parties. The operator controls how long local copies are kept and can delete them from the computer. The operator can revoke future Drive access in their Google Account connections.

This static site contains documentation and has no forms or analytics added by the app. Its hosting is subject to GitHub’s privacy statement.
